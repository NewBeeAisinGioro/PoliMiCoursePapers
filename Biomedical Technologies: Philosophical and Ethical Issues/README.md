### For 5 CFU students, they should decide whether to attend the group presentations or not. If so, they should write a review of max 2000 words. Otherwise, they should write an essay of max 4000 words. I chose not to attend the group presentations, so I wrote the essay. Finally, I got 30/30L for this course.<br></br>
#### Here are the assessment details for 5 CFU students:
#### With presentation:
Presentation (50% grade):
- work in a group of max 3 participants
- develop a case-study
- apply conceptual tools of the course
- develop a central claim and argument
- present the results in class in max 30 minutes

Literature Review (50% grade):
- work individually
- select 3 articles on the same topic from
the reading list
- give a very short summary of the arguments and claims
- compare and contrast their merits and limits
- write a review of max 2000 words
#### Without presentation:
Individual essay (100% grade):
- work individually
- develop a case study
- apply the conceptual tools of the course
- develop a central claim and argument
- write an essay of max 4000 words
<br></br>
